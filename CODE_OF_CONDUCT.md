# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This repository is dedicated to learning and collaborative growth. We pledge to maintain an inclusive, welcoming environment where everyone can ask questions, make mistakes, and learn without harassment, ridicule, or discrimination.

## Expected Standards

- Respect each other, even when technical disagreements arise.
- Critique the **code**, never attack the **author**.
- Assume good intent. Someone asking a basic question is learning, not being lazy.
- Receive and deliver feedback constructively.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insulting remarks, or derogatory language in any form.
- Personal attacks or discriminatory commentary based on gender, race, religion, sexual orientation, appearance, or proficiency.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Spam or off-topic self-promotion.

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you witness a violation, please contact project maintainers privately. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers reserve the right to remove non-compliant comments or commits and restrict offending accounts.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and public spaces where members represent the project.
